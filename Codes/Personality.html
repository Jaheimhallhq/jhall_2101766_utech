My Strongest Traits

Relentless Ambition

Within all my classes i try to go hard and never settle for just getting by or doing minimum to pass a class. If I encounter a complex network configuration that fails or an elusive bug during a lab my ambition drives me to to keep digging, troubleshooting and learning until I find a solution. It pushes me to go beyond lecture materials and constantly challenge myself.

Disciplined

My discipline gives me the structured mindset needed to succeed in my cybersecurity major. It keeps me organised through a lot of my lab assignments and projects, and that i always double check my work and maintain a consistent study habit rather than relying on last minute cramming.

Trustworthy

Being trustworthy is fundamental to who i am and a vital to the field of cybersecurity, where handling a lot of sensitive data. This trait shows up in how i collaborate with my classmates and approach to work. During group projects my peers can always depend on me to pull my weight and deliver results on time.

Who I look up to

Thomas Shelby

Thomas Shelby is a fictional character from the show Peaky Blinders and is a veteran of World war 1 he was born into a working class family in Birmingham England he was a tunneler in the trenches in the war and after he comes home he builds a business empire from scratch moving from bookmaker to a industrialist and to a member of Parliament in the House of Commons.

My favorite quote from Thomas is when when his background is questioned and he responds with Im not a traitor to my class Im just an extreme example of what a working man can achieve. The quote stuck with me because being from a lower class and now you have aquired wealth and status it doesnt mean that you have forgotten your roots or where you came from and also that even though one can be broken, down on his luck or simply of a lower class it doesn't mean he can't rise above because having ambition can show what a man can achieve.

Essay on LLMS

AI definitely can make learning faster and more convenient but even though I know that if I put too much of a dependence on it I am not going develop the skills and knowledge to complete whatever task I was supposed to do without it. So its better to use LLM's rather as a personal helper or tutor. Onaran (2024) explains that this approach help to better understand tasks to do than it just doing for her and as a student i could use the same method to better understand my assignments rather than it just doing it for me.

But also AI should not be used only for information Leanard's AI as a tool, not a crutch (2026) say that while AI can increase efficiency it doesn't necessarily increase quality. As many answers may appear correct but contains many inaccurate or information thats not complete so using textbooks, articles, and academic articles alongside AI is still things to be used. Traditional learning methods also help in better problem solving skills and help discover information rather just getting a fast answer from an LLM. So me using AI as a tool and as a is on me and it on me to know that, because if I rely on AI to do all my work it is completed yes but have I done the work and learnt what is actually to be done no, so its on me to use AI meaningfully and understand that I still need to do my own research and thinking.
